# My Career Vision, Core Values & Engineering Philosophy

My immediate career goal as I approach graduation in June 2027 is to excel in Software Quality Engineering (SQE), QA Automation, and AI-Integrated Full-Stack Development. I enjoy sitting at the intersection of building intelligent software and rigorously testing it to make sure it actually works under real-world pressure.

Three core engineering values guide how I work:
1. Practical Execution Over Pure Theory: I strongly believe that real learning happens when you ship working software. While university courses teach foundational terminology, mastering practical tools—like building an ERP in Next.js and Supabase, testing APIs, or deploying RAG pipelines—is what makes a developer genuinely valuable.
2. AI Vibe Coding with Discipline: I embrace modern AI coding assistants like Gemini Pro, Claude, Cursor, and Antigravity to accelerate boilerplate development. However, I believe a developer must understand system architecture, data security, and debugging so they can verify and test every line of AI-generated code.
3. Privacy and Honest Grounding: Whether designing multi-tenant encryption for Sialkot manufacturers in ERPify or building an AI digital clone, software must respect user privacy and never fabricate information when data is missing.
