# ERPify — System Architecture & Technical Deep Dive

My Final Year Project (FYP) at the University of Management and Technology (UMT) Sialkot Campus for the 2026-2027 session is titled "ERPify — AI-Assisted ERP for Local Manufacturing SMEs." I am building this platform alongside my teammates Muhammad Usman Tahir, Salman Shah, and Abdullah under the supervision of Mr. Saad Mansoor. Our target market consists of small and medium manufacturing and distribution businesses in Sialkot, particularly within the sports goods and surgical instruments sectors.

ERPify uses a specialized Two-Layer Architecture designed to balance reliability with artificial intelligence flexibility. The first layer is the Deterministic Core ERP. This layer runs on pre-tested, fixed business logic covering essential daily operations: inventory tracking, point-of-sale (POS) invoicing, procurement workflows, customer credit (udhaar) ledger management, and foundational accounting. Because financial calculations must never hallucinate, the Deterministic Core is strictly rule-based.

The second layer is the AI-Assisted Extension Layer. When a factory owner needs a custom module beyond the core features—such as a specialized Bill of Materials (BOM) calculator or a custom Delivery Tracking module—they do not need to hire a developer or write complex prompts. Instead, ERPify presents an AI-Driven Guided Questionnaire. The owner answers simple business questions, and our backend uses the Anthropic Claude API integrated via the Vercel AI SDK to generate the module automatically. Every AI-generated module is first executed and verified inside an isolated Sandbox and Validation Layer before reaching production.

For data privacy and multi-tenancy, we implemented Tenant-Level Encryption using a Zero-Knowledge Architecture so that not even system administrators can inspect a factory's plaintext financial records. Additionally, we enforce PostgreSQL Row-Level Security (RLS) in Supabase so each business tenant's data remains completely isolated.
