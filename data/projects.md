Currently in my 7th semester at the University of Management and Technology (UMT) Sialkot Campus, my primary focus is my Final Year Project (FYP) for the 2026-2027 session, titled "ERPify — AI-Assisted ERP for Local Manufacturing SMEs." I am developing this under the supervision of Mr. Saad Mansoor, alongside my teammates Muhammad Usman Tahir, Salman Shah, and Abdullah.

ERPify is a multi-tenant, cloud-based SaaS platform specifically targeted at small and medium manufacturing and distribution businesses in Sialkot, such as the sports goods and surgical instruments sectors. We designed it to be highly scalable and secure, offering a competitive edge over legacy local desktop applications (like C#/.NET apps) and expensive enterprise solutions like SAP.

The system is built on a Two-Layer Architecture. The first layer is the Deterministic Core, which handles fixed-logic operations like inventory, sales/POS, procurement, and customer credit management. The second is the AI-Assisted Extension Layer. Instead of writing complex prompts, business owners interact with an AI-driven guided questionnaire powered by the Claude API (Anthropic) and Vercel AI SDK. This allows the system to generate custom modules, such as Delivery Tracking or Bill of Materials, in the background. To maintain system stability, every AI-generated module is executed and validated in an isolated sandbox before reaching production.

Security is a major priority for ERPify. We implemented Tenant-Level Encryption (Zero-Knowledge Architecture) so that not even system administrators can view a tenant's plaintext financial data. Furthermore, we enforce Row-Level Security (RLS) to ensure strict data isolation between different factories. Our core tech stack consists of TypeScript and the Next.js framework for the UI and server logic, with Supabase (PostgreSQL) handling the database, authentication, and real-time features. We also utilize Docker, Git, and AI coding tools like Cursor for our development workflow.
