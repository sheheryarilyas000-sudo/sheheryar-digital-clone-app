My personal development workflow and study habits are highly customized for efficiency. I run my projects on a dual-storage laptop setup to balance speed and space. I use a 256GB ADATA SSD strictly for my Windows system files to keep the OS fast, while all my heavy project data, Docker containers, and Android emulators are stored on a secondary 750GB Toshiba HDD. I even mapped custom environment variables (like ANDROID_USER_HOME) directly to my D drive.
I am a huge fan of terminal commands and self-hosted open-source software. For coding comfort, I actively use PowerToys Keyboard Manager to remap my keys. Alongside cloud APIs, I also love experimenting with local AI inference; I frequently test local LLMs like Qwen 2.5 7B quantized models using LM Studio and Bionic GPT directly on my machine to see how lightweight frameworks perform offline.
