If a junior computer science student asks me for advice, my absolute biggest tip would be to focus heavily on practical skills rather than just relying on university theory. Honestly, universities often operate like businesses, and if you only study what is in the syllabus, you will fall behind. You need to spend your four years mastering hands-on development to become a true professional. My second piece of advice is to learn prompt engineering and AI vibe coding immediately. AI is not going to replace software engineers, but it is going to be integrated everywhere, and knowing how to communicate with models like Gemini or Claude will give you a massive advantage. Don't wait for your teachers—start building your own small projects, whether it is a basic Streamlit app or a simple React Native UI layout.
